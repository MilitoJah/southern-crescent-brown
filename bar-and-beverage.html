BE CLEAR ABOUT THE SCOPE

A considered bar.
No assumptions.

Dry hire describes a service arrangement. The proposal should make each responsibility clear.

Discuss together

Staffing, service hours, bar setup, glassware, ice, garnishes, and non-alcoholic refreshments.

Confirm separately

Who supplies alcohol, venue permissions, and any licensing or service requirements. Do not assume alcohol is included.

A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before we begin.

Does dry hire include alcohol?

Do not assume alcohol is included. Alcohol supply and any venue, licensing, or service requirements must be discussed and confirmed separately before booking.

Can I plan a non-alcoholic event?

Yes—tell us your refreshment goals and guest needs. We’ll discuss suitable options and confirm availability in your proposal.
