PRACTICAL PLANNING

A few details.
A clearer plan.

Bring these questions to your catering conversation. Confirm the arrangements for your gathering before booking.

How far ahead should I inquire?

Send your date, estimated guest count, location, and meal format when you begin planning. We’ll confirm availability and the ordering timeline for your gathering before booking.

Can I request delivery, setup, or staffed service?

Tell us which service you need and share any building access, parking, loading, or room setup requirements. Your proposal will confirm the agreed delivery and service arrangements.

Do you serve my office location?

We serve metro Atlanta and Georgia. Include your office or venue location in the inquiry so we can confirm coverage and the arrangements for that address.

How should I share dietary needs or allergies?

Include dietary requests, allergies, and the number of guests affected in the optional details. Discuss requirements with us before booking; available menu options and accommodations need to be confirmed.

What about pricing, payment, and invoicing?

Request a quote for your date, location, guest count, and service. If your organization needs an invoice, purchase order process, or specific payment arrangements, mention it in your inquiry so the terms can be discussed and confirmed before booking.
