Usage notes

Each shortlisted image is listed under the Pexels license in its source metadata. Pexels permits free commercial use and modification without required attribution. Do not imply that pictured people or brands endorse Southern Crescent. Check visible logos and other third-party rights before final publication. Avoid presenting stock scenes as past performance or actual government clients.

Dry hire bar imagery should describe the service accurately and should not suggest that alcohol is supplied. Generic conference imagery is a better fit for government audiences than seals, uniforms, or photos suggesting a specific agency is a client.
