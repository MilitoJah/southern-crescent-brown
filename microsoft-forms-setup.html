STEP 1 · MICROSOFT FORMS

Create and review your form.

  1. Download the Word document above. Open Microsoft Forms and sign in to the account that should own the inquiries.
  2. If Quick Import is available, choose Import from your device and select the document. Choose Form. Otherwise create a New Form and copy the questions.
  3. Review the imported questions. Use Date for Event date, Long answer for Special details, and a number restriction for Estimated guest count. Forms does not necessarily reproduce the custom website’s validation.
  4. Mark these 12 questions required: Name, Email, Phone, Address, Postal code, City, State, Event date, Event location, Estimated guest count, Estimated budget, and Referred by. Leave Company name, Service of interest, Start time, End time, and Special details optional.
  5. Under Collect responses, choose Anyone can respond so clients can use it without signing in. This option depends on your organization’s policy.
  6. Preview the form and submit a test. Use a confirmation such as: “Thank you. Your inquiry has been submitted. This does not confirm availability or a booking.”
  7. Copy the public response link from Collect responses and share it in the chat. Do not share an edit link, password, or access token.

STEP 2 · POWER AUTOMATE

Route each inquiry
to your business inbox.

  1. Open Power Automate. Create an Automated cloud flow called “Southern Crescent — New website inquiry.”
  2. Trigger: Microsoft Forms → When a new response is submitted. Select your new form.
  3. Add Microsoft Forms → Get response details. Select the same form; use Response Id from the trigger.
  4. Add Office 365 Outlook → Send an email (V2). Set To to hello@southerncrescenthospitality.com. Use your signed-in Outlook account as the sender.
  5. Subject: “Website inquiry — [Name] — [Event date]”. Insert the dynamic answers from Get response details.
  6. In the body, include all question answers with readable labels. If Reply To is available, use the answer to the Email question, rather than the built-in signed-in responder email.
  7. Save and test with a new response. Confirm the submission appears in Forms, the flow run succeeds, and the notification arrives in the inbox. Do not assume one proves the others.

STEP 3 · WEBSITE HANDOFF

Share the response link.
I’ll handle the embed.

Once your form is ready, I can replace the website’s draft-only form with an accessible Microsoft Forms embed and an open-form fallback link.

The surrounding page will retain Southern Crescent branding. The form fields themselves use Microsoft Forms styling. We’ll check external access, mobile display, confirmation behavior, and email delivery before calling the connection complete.

Inquiries will be stored in Microsoft 365 under the form owner’s account. Review your organization’s retention, access, licensing, and privacy requirements as part of setup.
