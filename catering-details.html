AT YOUR LOCATION

Delivery & setup

Service area & arrival

We serve metro Atlanta. Locations outside metro Atlanta are quoted separately. Delivery pricing is confirmed in your proposal.

Delivery is scheduled 30 minutes before your requested start time. Please provide an onsite contact, building access, and parking or loading instructions.

What setup includes

Setup with chafers and display is $75–$125, depending on size. We set up and label the food.

Moving furniture, audiovisual support, and cleanup are not included unless booked as part of staffed service.

Equipment & collection

Chafers and serving pieces are collected the same day or next business day. Missing or damaged equipment is billed at replacement cost.

Food handling

Follow the handling instructions and marked discard times provided with your order. Food must not be kept beyond its labelled discard time.

CARE FOR EVERY GUEST

Dietary requests & allergies

Share meal counts for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, and dairy-free requests, plus any allergies, when ordering. Menu suitability and accommodations must be confirmed before booking.

For orders placed in advance, dietary changes are accepted up to 3 business days before delivery. Confirm requirements for a short-notice order when placing it.

Meal labels

Each boxed meal is labelled with the person’s name or diet type, plus the major allergens it contains.

Severe allergies

Discuss severe allergies with us before booking. Our process for these requests uses individually packed, sealed meals, prepared first and kept separate during delivery.

Shared kitchen

Meals are prepared in a kitchen that also handles common allergens, including peanuts, tree nuts, wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, fish, shellfish, and sesame. We cannot guarantee allergen-free food.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Payment & invoicing

Corporate offices

One-time orders are paid by card when ordering. Approved repeat accounts may use Net 15 invoicing.

Government agencies

Purchase card at ordering, or Net 30 invoicing with a purchase order. No deposit is required for government agency orders.

Events & bar service

A 50% deposit books the date. The remaining balance is due 7 days before the event.

Recurring meals

Billed weekly or monthly by card on file or ACH.

Overdue private-client invoices incur a 1.5% monthly late-payment charge. Government payment requirements are handled under the applicable purchase order and terms.

IF PLANS CHANGE

Cancellations & closures

  • More than 72 hours before: full refund; event deposits are retained.
  • 24–72 hours before: 50% charge.
  • Under 24 hours before: full charge.
  • Government weather or emergency closures: rescheduling at no charge whenever possible.

Review the terms in your proposal before confirming your booking.
